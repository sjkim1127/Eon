§eon-zwds — Zi Wei Dou Shu Engine

Pure-Rust calculation engine for traditional Chinese Zi Wei Dou Shu astrology. Algorithm reference: SylarLong/iztro (TypeScript, MIT)

§Key Features

  • 12-palace chart placement calculation
  • 14(主星) + 6 + ~50
  • (四化: 化祿·化權·化科·化忌)
  • (大限, 10 ) / (流年, 1 )
  • (命主) / (身主)
