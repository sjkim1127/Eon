小限 (annual minor limit) and 斗君 (annual “Dou Jun” palace).

Both are pure palace look-ups for a target year; the chart-aware wrappers (crate::ZwdsChart::xiaoxian and crate::ZwdsChart::dou_jun) add names, stems and 四化.

§小限

《紫微斗數全書》安小限訣: 寅午戌人辰上起, 申子辰人自戌宮, 巳酉丑人未宮始, 亥卯未人起丑宮; 男順女逆, 一歲一宮. Age 1 (虛歲, nominal age counted from the lunar birth year) sits in the start palace; males then advance along the branch order (辰 → 巳 → 午 …), females go backwards.

§斗君

《紫微斗數全書》斗君訣: 以流年太歲所在宮為正月, 逆數至生月, 再從該宮起子時, 順數至生時, 即斗君. In ZWDS coordinates (寅 = 0): dou_jun = sui − (birth_month − 1) + hour_branch, where sui is the palace of the year branch and hour_branch is the standard hour index (子 = 0). Schools differ on leap months (here the leap month uses its month number) and on whether the late-子 hour (23:00–24:00) belongs to the next day; see docs/CALCULATION.md.
