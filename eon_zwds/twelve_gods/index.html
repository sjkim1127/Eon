Module twelve_gods 

Source
Expand description

The four “twelve-god” star groups of Zi Wei Dou Shu (十二神煞).

Each group occupies all twelve palaces, one god per palace:

GroupStarts atDirection
長生十二神by five-element bureau (水二/土五 → 申, 木三 → 亥, 金四 → 巳, 火六 → 寅)陽男陰女 順行, 陰男陽女 逆行
博士十二神the palace of 祿存陽男陰女 順行, 陰男陽女 逆行
將前十二神將星 by year branch (寅午戌 → 午, 申子辰 → 子, 巳酉丑 → 酉, 亥卯未 → 卯)always 順行
歲前十二神歲建 in the palace of the year branchalways 順行

順行 means following the branch order (寅 → 卯 → 辰 …), i.e. increasing PalaceIndex.

Sources: 《紫微斗數全書》 安長生十二神訣, 安博士十二神訣, 安將前諸星訣, 安歲前諸星訣; cross-checked against iztro (src/star/decorativeStar.ts, getchangsheng12, getBoShi12, getYearly12). Unlike the 14 major stars these gods are kept apart from crate::PalaceData::stars: they are a per-palace annotation (PalaceTwelveGods) and never take part in formation or 四化 logic.
