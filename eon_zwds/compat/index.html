合盤 (two-chart comparison) for Zi Wei Dou Shu.

This is a heuristic, not a classical verdict: there is no single canonical 合盤 score. The module reads two things from a pair of charts:

  1. 飛星 across charts: the stems of each person’s 命宮 and 夫妻宮 are flown into the other person’s chart (each stem gives 化祿·化權·化科·化忌, each landing on the palace that holds the transformed star in the other chart). This is the usual 合盤 technique (“A 的命宮化忌入 B 的 夫妻宮”).
  2. Star overlaps: stars that stand in both people’s 命宮/夫妻宮 (and across 命宮 ↔ 夫妻宮).

Scoring (harmony_score, 0–100): every flight contributes type_weight × palace_weight to either the supportive or the tense side, and the score is the balance 50 + 50 × (supportive − tense) / (supportive + tense) (50 when nothing lands).
