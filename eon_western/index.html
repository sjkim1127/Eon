eon-western: Western Astrology Engine

Utilizes Swiss Ephemeris FFI from eon-astro to calculate 10 major planetary bodies, True Node, South Node, Black Moon Lilith, and House Cusps (Placidus, Koch, Whole Sign, Equal House, etc.), major/minor aspects (applying/separating), Essential Dignities, House Rulership networks, Arabian Parts (Lot of Fortune/Spirit/Eros), geometric aspect patterns (Grand Trine, T-Square, Grand Cross, Yod, Kite), Synastry, Composite, and Secondary Progressions.
