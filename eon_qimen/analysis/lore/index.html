Source
Expand description

Classical lookup tables of 奇門遁甲: palace branches and elements, the auspice of doors, stars and deities, and the stem-over-stem formations (格局).

Sources are the common classical texts (《煙波釣叟歌》, 《奇門遁甲統宗》) as summarised in modern manuals. Schools disagree on the details, so every choice below is documented in docs/CALCULATION.md section 7a and the scores are heuristics, not verdicts.

deity_score
Auspice of a deity: 值符 (+2); 九天 九地 太陰 六合 (+1); 螣蛇 玄武 (-1); 白虎 (-2).
door_element
door_score
Auspice of a door: the three good doors 開休生 (+2), the three bad doors 傷驚死 (-2, death -3); 景 and 杜 are “middling” (0).
palace_branches
Earthly branches that sit in a palace (後天八卦 / Later Heaven arrangement).
palace_element
palace_of_branch
The palace a branch belongs to (never the centre).
punishment_palace
Palace where a heaven-plate stem is “punished” (六儀擊刑): 戊 in 震, 己 in 坤, 庚 in 艮, 辛 in 離, 壬 and 癸 in 巽.
star_score
Auspice of a star: 輔 心 (+2), 禽 任 沖 (+1), 英 柱 (-1), 蓬 芮 (-2).
temple_palace
Palace where a heaven-plate stem sits “in its own temple” (三奇升殿): 乙 in 震, 丙 in 離, 丁 in 兌.
tomb_palace
Palace where a three-wonder stem enters its tomb (三奇入墓): 乙 in 坤, 丙 in 乾, 丁 in 艮.
